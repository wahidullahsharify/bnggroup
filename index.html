About BNG Foundation

The Founder’s Message

"A commitment to excellence has always driven the Bashir Navid Group of Companies. It serves as a symbol of development in Afghanistan's business and economic growth. Over the decades, as a company, we've worked well by investing in commerce and trading worldwide. With the establishment of The Bashir Navid Group (B.N.G.) Foundation, we have been working and supporting our efforts with the same philosophy to help the poor since 1992 to the present. My life journey led me to the Bashir Navid Group (B.N.G.) Foundation, to witness firsthand the profound poverty challenges faced by communities, such as the lack of food, the absence of safe shelter, and the urgent need for education and healthcare in Afghanistan. This experience solidified my conviction that genuine welfare stems from standing alongside those in crisis and empowering them to rebuild their lives through acts of charity and humanitarian aid funded by the Bashir Navid Group. Today, by Allah’s mercy and the blessings of our supporters, we serve thousands. This foundation is not only a charity — it is a responsibility, a commitment to give where it matters most, to the Afghan people who are hopeless and helpless. “We stand with the people of Afghanistan to build a future defined by dignity, resilience, and hope. After all, I am humbled by what we’ve achieved, and I pray we continue to serve with sincerity and strength." You are cordially invited to explore our work and learn about our outstanding, successful charity projects nationwide. "

- Sincerely,
Al Haj Khairuddin Mayel Ahmadi
Founder & Chairman of Bashir Navid Group & B.N.G Foundation
Board of directors’ member at Balkh Chamber of Commerce and Investment (BCCI)

Our Mission

To empower communities through education, humanitarian aid, and development programs that promote dignity, self‑reliance, and long‑term well‑being.

Our Vision

A society where every individual, regardless of their background or hardship, has the opportunity to thrive with dignity and purpose.

Our Programs

What We Do

We provide comprehensive services in key areas to build a better future for communities in need.

Education

We pay school and exam fees, help students prepare for university, and support long-term learning and capacity building.

Humanitarian Aid

We provide grocery packages to over 3,000 people annually, distribute Iftar meals during Ramadan, and offer support cards for families in need.

Women Empowerment

We run vocational training programs (e.g., sewing) for widows and assist them in launching small businesses for independence.

Community Development

We support families in rebuilding their lives after crises and implement local programs to improve long-term community resilience.

Health Support

We provide assistance to patients with severe conditions, such as kidney disease, and help families access urgent medical treatment.

And much more...

Our work is constantly expanding to meet the needs of the communities we serve.
